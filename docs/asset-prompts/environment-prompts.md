# Environment Prompt Pack

## Ziel
Originale, IP-sichere Umwelt-Assets fuer `ShellRPG-www`, konsistent ueber Biome, Tageszeiten, Wetter und Perspektiven.

## Dateinamensschema
`[biome]_[timeofday]_[weather]_[view]_[variant].png`

## Stilregeln
- dark fantasy, grounded, photorealistic
- high detail, natural materials, muted palette
- strong readability from gameplay distance
- clear terrain silhouette
- no copyrighted interface frames
- no direct Diablo replication
- no logo, watermark, text, UI chrome

## Negative Prompts
- cartoon
- anime
- neon cyberpunk
- oversaturated fantasy
- toy-like miniatures
- floating UI elements
- isometric city builder style
- pixel art
- low-poly
- blur soup

## Basis-Prompt Topdown
`Top-down dark fantasy environment tile, photorealistic ground materials, strong terrain readability, moody cold palette, subtle atmospheric depth, tactical gameplay clarity, no characters, no UI, no text, no watermark`

## Basis-Prompt Birdseye
`Bird's-eye dark fantasy environment scene, grounded photorealism, eerie atmosphere, clear focal storytelling, dramatic but restrained light, gameplay-readable composition, no characters unless encounter-specific, no UI, no text, no watermark`

## Wetter-Modifikatoren
- `clear`: dry air, crisp edges, pale light, restrained haze
- `rain`: wet stone, reflective mud, darkened soil, subtle streak moisture
- `storm`: harsh sky energy, wind-scattered debris, tense lighting, darker contrast
- `fog`: dense low fog, softened background, clear foreground silhouettes
- `snow`: muted cold light, frosted edges, compressed contrast, visible surface accumulation
- `overcast`: flat cold daylight, suppressed highlights, melancholy tone

## Tageszeit-Modifikatoren
- `dawn`: cold blue-grey horizon with weak amber edge
- `day`: subdued desaturated daylight, not cheerful
- `dusk`: dying ember light, long shadows, brown-violet haze
- `night`: moonlit darkness, isolated highlights, deep silhouette contrast

## Biome-Spezifische Hinweise

### grassland
- sparse weeds, worn paths, wind-combed grass, damp earth

### forest
- roots, leaf rot, broken trunks, damp moss, filtered light

### swamp
- black water, slick mud, rotted reeds, fungal detail, mist pockets

### ruins
- cracked masonry, collapsed walls, ash, moss, weathered stone

### graveyard
- crooked stones, sunken graves, dead grass, iron fencing remnants

### rocky_wasteland
- shattered stone, dust crust, exposed mineral bands, harsh relief

### village_outskirts
- muddy cart tracks, old fences, neglected fields, cold domestic ruin
