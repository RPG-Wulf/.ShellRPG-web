# Encounter Prompt Pack

## Ziel
Originale birdseye Encounter- und Monster-Assets fuer das dominante CenterTile in `ShellRPG-www`.

## Dateinamensschema
`[biome]_[timeofday]_[weather]_[view]_[variant].png`

Beispiele:
- `encounter_ghoul_night_storm_birdseye_v01.png`
- `encounter_wolfpack_day_snow_birdseye_v02.png`

## Stilregeln
- photorealistic dark fantasy
- bird's-eye combat readability
- creature silhouette must be readable at a glance
- restrained gore, atmospheric menace over splatter
- tactical composition with a clear center of interest
- no copied monster silhouettes from existing ARPG IP

## Negative Prompts
- cartoon monster design
- exaggerated anime proportions
- comedic fantasy
- sci-fi armor or weapons
- glowing magic spam
- text, HUD, health bars, UI
- watermark, logo

## Basis-Prompt Encounter
`Bird's-eye dark fantasy encounter scene, photorealistic, strong focal creature silhouette, readable combat staging, moody environmental storytelling, restrained palette, no UI, no text, no watermark`

## Monster-Familien
- ghoul
- wolfpack
- revenant
- mire_beast
- ash_raider
- grave_knight
- carrion_flock
- marsh_hexer

## Wetter-Modifikatoren
- `rain`: wet fur, muddy footprints, reflective stone or soil
- `storm`: harsher contrast, wind pressure, violent cloud light
- `fog`: layered depth, softened edges around the field, crisp center silhouette
- `snow`: frosted fur/armor, compressed palette, visible cold breath when plausible
- `overcast`: muted light and flat dread

## Tageszeit-Modifikatoren
- `dawn`: weak horizon light, chilled surfaces
- `day`: cold desaturated daylight, still ominous
- `dusk`: ember-like edge lighting, heavy shadow volume
- `night`: moonlit contrast, selective highlight, deep blacks
